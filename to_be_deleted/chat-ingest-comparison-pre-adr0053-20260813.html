01 · executive brief

One useful rewrite. Four architectural reversals.

OpenCode improves conversation structure, ordering, and review visibility. But it also changes the canonical truth, replaces the accepted lane vocabulary, classifies too early, and drops the live source table.

CURRENT · FUNCTIONAL

PG-first, Phase-0 delivery

OPENCODE · UNCOMMITTED

Cleaner chat shape, wrong seam

Explicit conversations and ordering, but mixes raw truth, classification, human review, and sync state.

RECOMMENDED · PROPOSED

Preserve truth, derive meaning

Keep raw records canonical. Add ordering, outboxes, reviewed segments, provenance, and durable cursors.

Salvage these improvements

  • Explicit conversation metadata and stable message ordering.
  • New Gemini Markdown and custom-GPT parsers.
  • Thinking, attachments, and created works retained explicitly.

Blockers before merge

  • Classification must follow downstream chunking under ADR-0052.
  • The accepted six lanes cannot silently become five replacements.
  • Transactional full-row outboxes and durable per-sink cursors are missing.
