01

Evidence Operations Desk

Forensic queue + continuous custody jacket

Strength
Fast, court-conscious evidence triage with provenance and human gates always adjacent.
Cost / tradeoff
Focused rather than configurable; dense tables and complete inspector-state synchronization demand careful responsive work.
Best-fit workflow
Daily source review, custody exceptions, evidence drafting, authentication, redaction, and packaging.
Open prototype ↗ Review notes · PASS
02

Temporal Case Atlas

As-lived ↔ hindsight ↔ delta cartography

Strength
Makes the platform’s defining realization delta visible, explorable, and emotionally intelligible.
Cost / tradeoff
More specialized and interaction-heavy; exact provenance, safe replay execution, and tablet recomposition are substantial work.
Best-fit workflow
Walking horizons, comparing belief to later fact, inspecting realization boundaries, and explaining gaslighting deltas.
Open prototype ↗ Review notes · PASS
03

Modular Service Cockpit

Context spine + configurable service workspaces

Strength
Keeps hidden scope visible while exposing service ownership, providers, agents, ingestion, memory, and sandbox boundaries.
Cost / tradeoff
Highest governance burden, and this first prototype filters modules rather than proving true docking. Layouts, permissions, reset behavior, and context changes all need durable state and tests.
Best-fit workflow
Technical operations, ingestion supervision, agent/provider switching, service diagnostics, and isolated code work.
Open prototype ↗ Review notes · PASS after revision

These directions can coexist.

The comparison is not necessarily a single-winner exercise. A durable product could use the Evidence Desk as the daily case-work home, the Atlas as a focused analysis instrument, and selected Cockpit patterns—especially its context spine and provider controls—as shared platform infrastructure. The implementation decision is which surface becomes the primary shell and which become task-specific workspaces.
