# Workbench Mockup Review Board

> _Byline: Codex · GPT-5 · 2026-08-15_

Open `index.html` directly to compare three self-contained Operator Workbench
directions:

- `evidence-operations-desk/` — dense evidence review and custody provenance.
- `temporal-case-atlas/` — as-lived, hindsight, and realization-delta analysis.
- `modular-service-cockpit/` — configurable service workspaces with a persistent
  context spine.

The landing page is a neutral editorial review surface. It includes an optional
workflow lens, honest strength/cost/best-fit summaries, a comparison matrix,
direct relative links to each prototype, and links to their design-evaluation
notes under `docs/design/workbench-mockups-2026-08-15/`.

## Boundaries

- All variants use fictional sample data.
- They are visual prototypes, not deployed application screens or proof of safe
  backend execution.
- The review board and variants require no build step, external assets, or
  network access.
- The three variant directories remain independent and can be opened without
  the comparison page.

## Review posture

The directions do not have to collapse into one screen. A product can choose a
primary shell while retaining another direction as a task-specific instrument
or borrowing shared patterns such as the context spine, custody jacket, or
temporal delta ribbon.
