01 · accepted workflow

Preserve first. Chunk second. Classify third. Derive later.

WHOLE ARCHIVElogs + every generated work and attachment
COVERAGE ROUTERGo decoder or Python fallback
RAW CHATconversation + ordered messages; role retained
CANONICAL CHUNKmessage-safe, exact provenance
MULTI-LABEL + HITLconfidence decides auto vs review
PROJECTIONSone vector reused across eligible lanes
Nothing is discarded. Ambiguous or failed classification is immediately searchable in context and queued for review. A chat chunk can cross lanes, but it is stored and embedded once.
